NASA engineers prepared a space mission to study a planet beyond our solar system. The spacecraft will launch on a powerful rocket and travel into orbit around the distant world. A telescope on board will collect images and scientific data about the planet's atmosphere, surface, and possible moons. Mission planners checked the satellite communication system, navigation computers, and fuel supply before launch. Researchers hope the observations will help explain how planets form and whether life could exist elsewhere. The project includes a crew of astronomers and aerospace scientists who will monitor the flight from the ground. After the mission reaches orbit, the spacecraft will transmit its findings to Earth for analysis.
